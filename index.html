A little note for grown-ups

Start with MRT Adventure or the Primary 1–2 maths level. Older explorers can try fractions, percentages, real-world maps, and coordinate clues.

Every game offers gentle retries and sound controls. Treasure Trails uses arrow buttons or keyboard arrows, followed by “Dig here”. The other new games use three large choices.

The maths game practises selected primary skills; it isn’t a complete syllabus course. Sound and completed adventures stay in this browser. No sign-up needed.
