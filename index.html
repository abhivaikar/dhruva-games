A little note for grown-ups

Start with MRT Adventure or the Primary 1–2 maths level. Older explorers can try fractions, percentages, real-world maps, and coordinate clues.

Every game offers gentle retries and sound controls. Treasure Trails lets children tap a destination to walk, collect keys, rescue a turtle and solve a shape-lock puzzle. Arrow buttons and keyboard arrows also work. Colouring Studio offers brushes, fill colours and stamps. Dot-to-dot pictures can become colouring pages; saved artwork can become puzzles. The quiz games use three large choices.

The maths game practises selected primary skills; it isn’t a complete syllabus course. Sound and completed adventures stay in this browser. No sign-up needed.
