Little steps in a new language

Start with Listen & find. Each visit has just six words, or three speaking/sentence turns. Choose a familiar world and repeat favourites. Word detective and Sentence puzzles offer optional reading challenges.

All 84 words and 40 phrases in each game include bundled language audio. The Voice control changes the English guide; the learning clips have their own Mandarin or Marathi voice. The sound guide and English meanings are optional help, rather than a test of reading ability.

My speaking turn encourages trying a phrase aloud. Optional recording stays in memory on this device and is cleared when you change activity. It does not grade pronunciation or upload a recording. Passport counts record particular activities practised, not language proficiency.

These original beginner activities are not a complete school syllabus. Chinese uses simplified characters and Pinyin. Marathi uses Devanagari; colour words shown alone may change their endings in sentences. Play together and use a phrase away from the screen too.
